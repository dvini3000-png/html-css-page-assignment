About WeGo-2-You Auto Care

Skip the hassle of the traditional repair shop! WeGo-2-You Auto Care brings certified, dealership-quality automotive services right to your doorstep. At home, your office or even on the roadside. We understand that your time is extremely valuable, which is why we fix your vehicle right where it stands

Whether you need routine maintenance or troubleshooting, our goal is to deliver honest, transparent and premium service without disrupting your busy day. We proudly service all major makes and models of vehicles.

We use premium, industry-certified replacement parts sourced directly from authorized dealers. Learn more about professional auto motive standards at the National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence.

Our Core Mobile Services

Our fully equipped mobile service van handles a wide range of repairs on-site:

  • Brake Pad, Rotor and Shoe Replacement
  • Battery Testing & On-Site Installation
  • Routine Lubrication, Fluid Top-Ups & Oil Changes
  • Alternator, Starter, Belt Repairs & Tune-Ups
